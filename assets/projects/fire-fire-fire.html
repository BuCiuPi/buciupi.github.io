Fire Fire Fire

Unity Engine

Hyper Casual, Puzzle Solving



FireFireFire is a physics-based puzzle game, your goal is to strategically stack objects and use fire to burn the target. Carefully arrange the available items, taking into account their shapes, weight, and balance, to create a stable structure. Once your stack is ready, ignite the fire and watch as it spreads, aiming to burn the target.

I maintaining and expand this game during the time at Indigames Inc as a Gameplay, UX/UI Programmer.

Published by GMO a Web game platform.





Fire Fire Fire

  1. GamePlay Details

    • In this puzzle game, players must skillfully stack a variety of objects to create a stable structure that allows fire to reach and burn the target.
    • Each level provides a set of items—such as wooden blocks, metal beams, and flammable objects—with different weights and properties. Players must drag and drop these items, balancing them carefully to prevent the stack from collapsing.
    • The challenge lies in ensuring the fire spreads to the target without burning out too quickly or missing the mark.
