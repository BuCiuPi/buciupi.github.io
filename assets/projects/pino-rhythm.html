Pino Rhythm

Unity Engine

Rhythm, Music



Tap to the rhythm as ice cream cubes drop, matching the beat perfectly to keep the combo going and rack up high scores.

Published by Morinaga Milk . The game can be played by purchasing a Pino Ice Cream during a time-limited event.
