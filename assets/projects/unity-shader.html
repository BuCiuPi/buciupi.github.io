In Unity, ShaderLab enables developers to write custom shaders with HLSL for precise rendering control, while Shader Graph provides a visual, node-based alternative for artists to craft shaders easily; together, they power Effects and VFX like smoke, fire, and magical spells, often built with Unity's Particle System and VFX Graph for creating high-quality, GPU-driven visual effects that enhance the game's visual storytelling and atmosphere.
